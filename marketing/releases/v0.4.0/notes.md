## Highlights

### Milestones, cycles and estimates

Plan a project with milestones and cycles, and give tasks estimates. The milestone timeline shows each milestone's progress in tasks and points, with the cycles on top. Tasks carried over to the next cycle stay in the cycle's progress. (#113, #129)

![Milestone timeline](https://github.com/coollabsio/orbit/releases/download/<tag>/screenshot-milestones.png)

| Cycles | Recurring tasks |
| --- | --- |
| ![Project cycles](https://github.com/coollabsio/orbit/releases/download/<tag>/screenshot-cycles.png) | ![Repeat settings of a task](https://github.com/coollabsio/orbit/releases/download/<tag>/screenshot-recurring.png) |

Completing a recurring task creates the next occurrence.
